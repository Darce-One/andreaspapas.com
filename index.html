Welcome

Hi! I'm Andreas, and this corner of the internet is my digital home.

Everything I do, I do for the love of music. By day, I'm a software developer and researcher at Steinberg Media Technologies. By night, I'm a hobbyist piano player and oud learner.

I enjoy bridging the gap between computing and music. As a student, I created many projects, documented in the projects page. I don't write often, but when I do, I publish them in the writings page.

I love sharing and discovering music. To this end, you'll find my music recommendations in the music share page. If you discover a new album you love, let me know, and share one of your own favourites!

Happy browsing!
