## Tabbouleh: Granular Audio Effect

I built an audio effect VST that music producers can plug-in to their digital audio workstations, to give their music some extra spice. As a fan of granular synthesis, I built a granulator and based the parameters off my favourite salad, the tabbouleh.

The recipe has a few ingredients that translate into effect parameters:

- Parsley maps to the audio ‘grains’.
- Tomatoes map to basic synth grains.
- Oil maps to reverberation.

You get the picture.

!youtube[Tabbouleh Granular Plugin](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xU8_yCE11FM)

I did not design a UI for it, and opted to use the basic JUCE default. If it is something you would like to try out, check back later: there will be a download link on this page soon.
