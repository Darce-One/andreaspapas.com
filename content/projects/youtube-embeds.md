---
title: Markdown video embeds
date: 2026-09-15
description: A working example of embedding a YouTube video in a project or writing.
---

Pages can include YouTube videos directly from their Markdown source. Use this syntax on its own line:

`!youtube[Optional video title](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID)`

!youtube[Example YouTube embed](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ)
