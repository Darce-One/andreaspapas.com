During my time as a work-student at [Steinberg Media Technologies GmbH](https://www.steinberg.net/), I built [libmusictok](https://github.com/steinbergmedia/libmusictok), a symbolic music tokenisation library for C++. libmusictok is designed to be compatible with [MIDITok](https://miditok.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html), such that tokenisers configured in python can be directly used in cpp applications. This enables builders to deploy their symbolic music models with ease.

Visit the [GitHub repository](https://github.com/steinbergmedia/libmusictok) to learn how to include it in your own projects.
